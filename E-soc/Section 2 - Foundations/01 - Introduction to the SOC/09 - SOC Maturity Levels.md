
## What is SOC Maturity ?

- SOC Maturity is basically how well the SOC is performing
- Its used to measure what the SOC'S level in an organizations
- it used to measure the capability and effectiveness of the soc 
  
  ![](Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704130725.png)


## why Maturity matters 

- if we know soc's maturity level, we can determine how well soc responds to  an incident
- it enables organizations to know what the soc team is good and bad at.
-  we can measure the progress of the soc team
